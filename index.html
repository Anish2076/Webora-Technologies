ANSWERS BEFORE WE BEGIN

Frequently asked
questions.

Have another question? Send a project enquiry and tell me what you have in mind.

Ask about your project ↗
What services do you offer?

Website development, responsive design, UI/UX design and website refreshes for individuals, startups and businesses.

How is project pricing decided?

Every project is different. The quote is based on your goals, pages and required features; the scope and quote are discussed before work begins.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the project scope and the feedback process. We agree on a timeline during planning before development begins.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive layouts are planned for mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you refresh an existing website?

Yes. Website refreshes can improve a site's structure, visual style and mobile experience.

How do I get started?

Send a project enquiry with your goals and requirements. We can then discuss the scope, timeline and next steps.
